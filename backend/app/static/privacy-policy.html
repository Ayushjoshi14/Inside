Privacy Policy

App: Inside — Know What's Inside

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Inside may collect account information such as your email address, information you provide in your profile, ingredient scan results, scan history, and premium purchase verification details.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to provide account access, analyze food ingredients, personalize results, maintain scan history, manage premium access, and improve app reliability.

3. Camera and Photos

The app may request camera access or photo-library access when you choose to scan or upload a product label. Images are processed to extract ingredient information. Do not scan or upload information you do not wish to share.

4. AI Analysis

Ingredient analysis may use third-party AI services to process submitted ingredient information. Results are informational and should not be treated as medical advice or a substitute for professional dietary guidance.

5. Payments

Premium purchases may be processed through Razorpay. Payment information is handled by the payment provider according to its policies. Inside may store transaction references and payment-verification records needed to manage premium access.

6. Data Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, database storage, AI analysis, and payment processing. We do not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

7. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures to protect stored information. Data is retained as needed to provide the service, maintain records, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You can choose whether to grant camera or photo access. For requests to access, correct, or delete your account information, contact us using the email address published on the app's official store listing.

9. Children's Privacy

Inside is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version will be published at the same privacy-policy URL.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through the contact email published on Inside's official store listing.
