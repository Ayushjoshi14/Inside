Privacy Policy

Understand how information is handled when you use Inside.

Effective date: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Inside – Ingredient Scanner (“Inside”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the app.

Please note: This policy should be kept accurate to the app's actual implementation and the services it uses. Review the data-processing details below before relying on this page for store compliance.

1. Information the app may process

  • Camera and selected photos: If you use the scanner or choose an image, the app accesses the camera or the image you select for ingredient-label scanning. Camera access is used for this feature.
  • Ingredient-label content: Text extracted from a label and information needed to analyze the product may be processed to provide ingredient explanations and a product score.
  • Scan history: The app may keep scan records such as product details, category, score, and date/time so you can view previous scans.
  • Payment information: If you purchase premium access, the payment is handled through Razorpay. We do not intend to collect or store your full card number, UPI PIN, or banking password. Razorpay and your payment provider handle payment credentials under their own policies.
  • Technical information: Our service provider or backend may process limited technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How information is used

Information is used to scan and analyze ingredient labels, calculate and display product results, maintain scan history, provide premium access, process or verify payments, and maintain the security and reliability of the app.

3. OCR, AI analysis, and service providers

To provide ingredient analysis, label images and/or extracted text may be processed by our backend and third-party OCR or AI services. These providers process information as needed to return analysis results and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices. Confirm which services receive images or text and update this section if necessary.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate scanning, AI analysis, hosting, security, or payment verification, or when required by law. Payment transactions are handled by Razorpay and relevant payment networks/providers.

5. Storage and retention

Scan history and related information may be stored on your device and/or our backend, depending on the app version and feature. Information is retained only for as long as needed to provide the relevant features, maintain records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Confirm the actual storage location and deletion process before publishing.

6. Permissions and your choices

You can deny camera access and may still be able to use image selection if that feature is available. You can stop using the app at any time. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored data, but it may not delete information already stored on a server or held by a payment provider.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children’s privacy

Inside is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Third-party services

Third-party services used by the app, including payment, hosting, OCR, or AI providers, may have their own privacy policies. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
